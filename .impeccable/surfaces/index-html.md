# Home page (index.html)

Scope: home page, Persuade mode. Related: about.html, contact.html (same world).

Audience: Florida owners of older homes, vacant land, tired commercial buildings, and acreage, mostly arriving on phones from Instagram adverts. Job: find out whether the land is worth more than what sits on it, and what the free services cost. Action: book a free analysis via contact.html. Proof on hand: real site plans, before/after images, completed communities. Constraints: black replaces green, Arquitecta only, no em or en dashes, all CTAs to contact.html, reduced-motion safe rotator.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The land is the asset. The page is a row of development-site signboards standing on real aerial photography: black boards, white capitals, one fact per board. It refuses the brokerage default of a white hero with gradient overlay and four icon cards.

OWN-WORLD: Black #0A0A0A boards, white #FFFFFF ground, one paper neutral #EDEBE6 for site plans. Arquitecta Heavy caps, tracked, on boards; Arquitecta Regular body. Hard rectangles, no radius, 1px rules, the key-mark as the only icon. Photography full-bleed, boards at hard edges.

STORY: The owner finds their property type named on a board, learns the free services are the price of a listing, sees real plans and built communities, and books a free analysis.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed aerial of lots under construction. Bottom-left black board, 46% wide: mark, "YOUR LAND IS WORTH MORE THAN WHAT SITS ON IT." in Heavy caps, one sentence, white "Book a free analysis" button. Logo top-left, two nav links top-right. The rotating property-type phrase lives on the board's top line.

FORM: Development-site signboard, candidate 6 of 7 on the grounded list, seed 0416eefe, code-led.

RAISES: Loteria: every photo captioned with its project name. Football kit: strict inversion, black board on white page, white board on black section, mark unchanged. Brick instructions: free services as three numbered steps showing the real deliverables. Oscilloscope: measured figures in tabular numerals against a rule. Black hole: the hero board keeps unmarked black. Minihompy: track record as a ledger of named projects.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
