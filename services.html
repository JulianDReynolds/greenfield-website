Services

Older homes. New possibilities

Homes with limited remaining use but strong underlying land value. We fully investigate each site's development potential and market the property on that basis, not just its current condition. By creating genuine competition among developers and builders, we give owners certainty that they are receiving the highest and best offer available in the market.

Vacant land

Infill lots and undeveloped acreage across Florida. Our expertise in zoning and entitlements ensures accurate valuation and positioning, so land is priced on what can be built rather than what sits there today.

Commercial redevelopment sites

Underperforming retail and office properties with redevelopment or repositioning potential. We identify the exit strategy the traditional market misses, and connect these assets with the buyers actively seeking them.

Master planned communities

For owners of major acreage across Florida, we bring entitlement and development expertise to bear on maximizing land value. We evaluate the highest-and-best use for large tracts, guide owners through the entitlement process, and partner them with best-in-class community developers. We structure transactions and joint ventures that capture the full development value of the land, not just its raw acreage price.
