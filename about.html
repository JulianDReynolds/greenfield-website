About Greenfield Group

Greenfield Group is a Florida real estate practice focused on development properties. We help owners navigate the sale of hard-to-value assets and help developers privately connect with property owners ahead of the broader market.

Our team brings more than 20 years of hands-on development experience, including the entitlement, development, and sale of approximately 2,000 residential lots and the delivery of roughly 600 condominium units. We have negotiated complex development, disposition, and joint venture transactions on both sides of the table. This means we value property the way buyers do, and negotiate the way owners need.

That development background is our edge: we don't just list property, we underwrite it. Every site is evaluated for zoning, entitlements, utilities, and highest-and-best use before it goes to market.
