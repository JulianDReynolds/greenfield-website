## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS, and vanilla JS, deployed by GitHub Pages from the `main` branch to greenfieldgroupfl.com. No build step. Redesign work lives on the `redesign` branch and must not touch `main` until the client approves.

## Users

Primary (confirmed 2026-09-02): Florida property owners deciding whether to sell an older home, vacant land, a tired commercial building, or major acreage. They are not developers; they suspect the land is worth more than what sits on it and want proof before they commit to a broker.

Secondary: developers and builders looking for their next site, who read the same pages to judge whether this broker understands entitlements and yield.

## Product Purpose

Greenfield Group is a Florida real estate practice (Boca Raton) that sells development property. It helps owners sell hard-to-value assets for what a developer would pay, and helps developers privately connect with owners ahead of the broader market. Success is an owner booking a free analysis through the contact form or calling.

## Positioning

The mechanism (confirmed 2026-09-02): free development services in exchange for the listing. Depending on the property, Greenfield provides a free architect's concept drawing, a free site plan, and a free engineer's site analysis, then markets the property on its development yield rather than its current condition or per-acre price. The team has more than 20 years of development experience (about 2,000 residential lots entitled, developed, and sold; about 600 condominium units delivered), so it values property the way buyers do.

Four service lines, each with an approved advert headline:

- Older homes. New possibilities: "Your property is a developer's dream." Free development analysis and architect's diagram when you list.
- Vacant land: "Priced by the acre, sold by the lot. Keep the difference." Book your free analysis.
- Commercial redevelopment sites: "Stop valuing the rent roll. Start valuing the land." Site plan, architect's concept, engineer's site analysis, all free with your listing.
- Master planned communities: "Don't sell your land to the middleman. Be the middleman." Free entitlement management for select properties.

## Product Principles

- Lead with the owner's question ("is my land worth more than my building?") and answer it with proof, not adjectives.
- The free services are the offer; they appear early and unmistakably on every page.
- Show real work: site plans, before and after, aerials. One decisive image beats five.
- Every path ends at the contact form; nothing else competes for the click.
- Plain, confident language. No jargon the owner would have to look up.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Reduced-motion safe rotation and reveals. Mobile first: most visitors come from Instagram on a phone.
